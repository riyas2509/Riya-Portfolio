# Creative-direction review: from portfolio components to an Idea Atlas

The previous site had a strong central idea and working interactions, but its visual grammar changed after the hero. The patterns below are common in generated portfolios. That is a design judgement about recognizability, not a claim about how any individual component was authored.

## Visual grammar

- **Paths have a job.** They connect process stages, project records, dates, interests, methods or a contact endpoint. They do not represent invented dependencies or technical architecture.
- **Five instruments are the alphabet.** The discovery lens, research layers, design frames, modular build and evolving branch recur as consistent line drawings. Desktop retains the procedural 3D versions.
- **Two surfaces, one system.** Deep blue-green is the exploratory sky; warm paper holds project evidence. Muted violet, sky blue, pink, mint and peach identify instruments or individual project diagrams rather than fill generic cards.
- **Typography carries hierarchy.** Sans-serif establishes the hero; roman serif titles introduce files and observations; monospaced labels identify scope, sequence and evidence. The repeated italic heading formula was removed. No external font requests were introduced.
- **Illustration is identified.** Project diagrams are interpretations of the supplied vision or research scope. They are not fabricated product screenshots, validated user flows or verified backend architecture.

## Accessibility and performance constraints

All existing routes, mailto and profile links, factual statuses and case-study content are preserved. Interactions use native buttons, links and disclosures, with visible focus and selected-state cues beyond color. The skip-link target is programmatically focusable. Decorative SVGs are hidden from assistive technology; nearby captions describe their purpose.

The revised visuals use local SVG, CSS and the existing lazy-loaded scene. No packages, remote fonts, downloaded images, post-processing passes or additional canvases were added. The scene retains its mobile alternative, reduced-motion logic, error boundary and offscreen rendering pause. No scroll interception was introduced.

The design intentionally keeps asset slots honest until real screenshots, prototypes and resume PDFs exist. It does not add results, credentials or commercial traction.
